# Privacy – Lucent Video

Lucent Video is a Kodi add-on. It has no servers of its own, no accounts of its own and no tracking or analytics.

## What stays on your device
- Your settings, sign-in tokens and keys are stored only in Kodi's own add-on folder on your device.
- Saved lists, progress and subtitles that Lucent Video keeps for speed are stored on your device too.
- Lucent Video's log lines never contain your keys, passwords, tokens or addresses. The error report removes them as well.

## What is sent, and only to services you choose
Lucent Video only talks to a service when you use a feature that needs it:
- **Your watch services** (Trakt, Simkl, PunchPlay, Wetrakr) – only after you sign in. They receive what you watch, your progress, ratings and list changes, so your history stays up to date.
- **TMDB** (and TVDB, Trakt for missing episode titles) – titles and pictures are looked up by their ID.
- **Your own source addresses** (AIOStreams, debrid or Usenet services you add) – the title you want to play.
- **Subtitle services** you switch on – the title and episode you search a subtitle for.
- **AI translation** (only if you add your own key) – the subtitle text to translate.
- **TheIntroDB** – the title and episode, to find the intro and credits.
- **Homey** (only if you set it up) – a short signal when a film or episode starts, pauses, goes on or ends, to your own Homey.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

## Signing out
You can sign out of each service in Lucent Video's settings. That removes the token from your device. Removing the add-on removes all its data from your device.
